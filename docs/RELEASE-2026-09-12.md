# Sam trust release — 12 September 2026

The user authorized implementation and deployment with “ok lets do the changes”, following the
product decisions in REVIEW-2026-09-11.md. This supersedes the earlier review-only boundary.

## Implemented

- Source-contribution workout journal: append follow-up details, preserve separate sessions, validate
  counts/grades against current evidence, keep missing quantities unknown, audit corrections and edits.
- Group conversation includes named speakers and replied-to text. Banter is not a training goal.
- Private raw messages, memories, facts, plans and free-text workout notes are excluded from group
  retrieval. Numeric training records can be shared. Backlog context also respects author/audience.
- A bounded, athlete/chat-specific pending question links an answer back to the session.
- Plans are saved from the same structured object that is rendered in the reply, not re-extracted
  from assistant prose. Assistant messages do not become evidence of completed workouts.
- Both athletes have the shared approximate trip; Alexey's individual target is selected guided
  outdoor 6a+ sections, with solo gym access and 2–3 sessions/week. Andrey has no invented grade target.
- Deterministic 7/28/90-day comparisons, route-length coverage, completed vs attempted highest grades,
  and paginated journal output work beyond 100 sessions.
- Durable intake precedes media/model operations. Separate retryable outbox, guarded edits, terminal
  failure accounting, restart recovery and database/worker health checks.
- Circular notes are transcribed, explicitly requested videos/documents get sampled-frame observations,
  oversized video gets an actionable explanation; media observations cannot themselves ground journal metrics.
- Isolated test credentials, secret-free settings repr, tzdata, tested dependency constraints and a
  Docker context that excludes local secrets, backups and virtual environments.

## Honest operational limits

Keep one polling machine. No Telegram exactly-once guarantee exists if the network loses a successful
send acknowledgment; the outbox avoids normal reprocessing duplicates but cannot eliminate that case.
An ambiguous correction asks for a target. Old merged records without contribution ownership cannot
always be safely undone automatically. Video analysis is sampled-frame analysis and the hosted Bot
API download limit remains 20 MB. Screenshot metric extraction, calendar/wearable integrations,
scheduled proactive coaching and an external failure pager are not part of this release.

Daily snapshots are enabled and a dump restore was tested; continuous off-site backups and high
availability are not configured. Future grade/volume claims still require trustworthy input, and
gym improvements do not prove readiness to lead outdoors.

The Telegram token previously printed by the old test fixture still requires rotation through the
owner’s BotFather account. This release prevents that fixture/settings exposure but cannot revoke a
token on the owner's behalf without access to that account. Do not paste a replacement token into chat.
