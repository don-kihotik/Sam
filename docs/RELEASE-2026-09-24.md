The same count remains after deployment; they were neither erased nor replayed. No new
runtime/media errors appeared in post-deployment checks. This release does not claim to
have recovered every historical failed attachment. The supplied catalog itself was loaded
from the already reviewed 70-card inventory.

Health/model/database checks do not substitute for a user-originated end-to-end Telegram
message. No synthetic workout was written to production and no test response was sent
to a chat. The owner can try `/history` in the personal chat for a deterministic smoke check.

The former image `registry.fly.io/sam-climbing:deployment-01M2BMZKDCK9ZVGRK2TS7YZZ7A`
is recorded for incident recovery, but reverting to it would also revert the self-only
policy. Do not blindly downgrade a used training schema: new feature data would be lost.
Take a fresh backup and choose a recovery plan before any rollback with data changes.
